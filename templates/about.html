Frequently Asked Questions

What formats does FlexiParse support?
FlexiParse currently specializes in all PDF documents, including scanned documents with text layers, research papers, legal briefs, textbooks, and technical documentation.
Can I export my chat conversations?
Yes! With a single click of the Export button in the workspace, your entire conversation history is downloaded as a beautifully formatted Markdown file complete with timestamps.
Does FlexiParse support voice queries and audio speech?
Absolutely. Click the microphone icon next to the chat bar to dictate questions hands-free, and click "Speak" on any AI response to hear the answer read aloud.
